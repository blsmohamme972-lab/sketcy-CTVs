This is an invented story for a nonmedical narration test. At sunset, a tiny librarian arrives at an imaginary library. A blue lantern beside the entrance politely bows its handle and invites the visitor through the door. Its square glass panels glow as though they are smiling. The lantern promises to remember where every visit begins.

Inside, the librarian meets three paper birds. One bird folds its wings, another performs a loop, and the third carries a tiny bookmark. All three circle a round clock hanging above the reading table. The clock taps a gentle rhythm while the birds count their circles. The librarian watches each bird before continuing across the room.

On the windowsill, a wooden boat is preparing for a very small voyage. It raises one white sail and waves its red flag. Although the boat never leaves the window, it announces that the journey is complete. The librarian laughs and looks back across the room.

First comes the blue lantern at the entrance. Next comes the clock above the reading table, with exactly three paper birds. Finally comes the boat on the windowsill, carrying one white sail and a red flag. The objects settle down as the imaginary library closes for the night.
